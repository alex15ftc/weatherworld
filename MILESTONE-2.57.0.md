# Milestone 2.57.0 — Storm-Track and Hazard Projection Engine

- Creates discrete forecast-storm candidates from initiation, occupancy, confidence, and corridor support.
- Projects mode-aware tracks and expected lifetimes.
- Accumulates tornado, hail, and wind probabilities from hazard swaths rather than broad environmental fields.
- Retains only a small background contribution from direct ingredient guidance.
- Stores candidate counts, swath attribution, and per-cell projection support in issued outlook products.
- Preserves existing SPC probability quantization, CIG rules, contour nesting, and 25-mile probability semantics.
