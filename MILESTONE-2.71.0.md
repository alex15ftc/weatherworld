# WeatherWorld 2.71.0 — Analog Scenario Intelligence

Adds a compact, non-authoritative analog critic. The engine classifies the active setup family, compares boundary motion, parcel placement, initiation timing, storm spacing, and convective mode against regime-family expectation ranges, and emits plausibility/confidence telemetry. Analogs never move boundaries, create storms, or directly promote risk tiers. Their confidence contribution is deliberately capped and only influences forecast predictability modestly.
