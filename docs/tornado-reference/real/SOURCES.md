## Cross-references

- Front-lit white funnels: 03_stovepipe_a, 04_rope_b, 01_cone_a (partly).
- Debris and dust at the base also show in 01_cone_a, 01_cone_b, 05_drillbit_c and 06_multivortex_a.
- Rain-wrapped or low-contrast: 01_cone_d, 02_wedge_c, 02_wedge_d.

## Gaps

- **Helical striations on the funnel itself**: no clean freely-licensed close-up was found. 10_* show the banding on the wall cloud and mesocyclone, not on the condensation tube. 03_stovepipe_a has the best visible wall texture.
- **Multiple-vortex**: only older or distant examples (a 1957 B/W photo, a 1974 video frame, a distant 2020s shot). There is no crisp modern photo of suction vortices around a wedge.
- **Debris ball close-up**: 08_debris_a is small (568 px).
- Several NOAA scans are grainy film with colour casts (01_cone_b, 04_rope_d, 07_funnel_a, 09_backlit_a). Use them for shape and value, not colour.
- 02_wedge_a and 08_debris_a have an SPC caption box burned in. 01_cone_c has a camera date stamp.
