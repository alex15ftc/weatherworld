# Storm Lifecycle

## Purpose
Storm objects realize convection from the authoritative environment. They are not prewritten hazard markers.

## Lifecycle
1. Candidate initiation.
2. Failed attempt, pulse convection or sustained updraft.
3. Organization into discrete, clustered or linear modes.
4. Mature hazard production.
5. Mode transition, merger, cycling or decay.

## Governing inputs
- Convective initiation probability and cap-break timing.
- Buoyancy, inhibition, lapse rates and moisture depth.
- Deep-layer and low-level shear.
- Storm-relative helicity and storm motion.
- Boundary-relative inflow and forcing overlap.
- Cold-pool strength and storm interactions.

## Design rules
- Potential severity and realized opportunity remain distinct.
- Tornadogenesis may follow fast or delayed pathways.
- Hazard output must be conditional on storm existence and organization.
- Storm motion should emerge from winds and propagation rather than fixed directional templates.
