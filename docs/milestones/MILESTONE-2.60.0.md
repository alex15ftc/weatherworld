# WeatherWorld 2.60.0 — Storm Evolution & Track Intelligence

## Scope

- dynamically recomputed storm motion with boundary-following and instability-attraction tendencies;
- mesocyclone-cycle motion deviations for curved and cyclic supercell tracks;
- persistent track confidence with 50% and 90% uncertainty corridors;
- retained storm motion and boundary-interaction history;

The atmospheric and probabilistic outlook engines remain intact. This milestone improves the realized and projected storm behavior consumed by those systems.
