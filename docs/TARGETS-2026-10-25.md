stay the strongest source.

## Why the days are lopsided (2026-10-25, later)

80 days (seeds 1-8), split into thirds by severe storm-hours:

| | Quietest | Middle | Busiest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh storms | 28 | 54 | 112 |
| Severe storms | 3 | 21 | 81 |
| Severe storm-hours | 2 | 39 | 222 |
| A severe storm's time severe | 0.2 h | 1.5 h | 2.5 h |
| Moist air (Td 60 F+) at 4 pm | 15% of the map | 42% | 66% |
| CAPE / MLCIN medians | 1,044 / 60 | 1,613 / 20 | 2,191 / 3 |

On the busiest days the lid is warm (11.6 C at 700 mb), but the ground air at 26.7 / 19.5 C lifts to about 12 C at
700 mb against 8.1 C there, so nothing holds the storms back over two thirds of the map.

Tried and reverted:
- **The night lift's cap cooling given its own 6 h memory** (`mesoCapLiftHours`). The busiest days stayed at CIN 3;
  the quietest went from 60 to 131.
- **A warm-sector aim in the region steering:** a proxy of the moist share from the outer model's surface moisture
  (correlation 0.60 with the map's), aimed at 0.6 and weighted 0.5. The moist-share spread barely moved, from p10 /
  p50 / p90 of 6 / 43 / 79% to 5 / 48 / 79%; the slide is limited to the systems' speed and 40% of days aim at
  lows.
