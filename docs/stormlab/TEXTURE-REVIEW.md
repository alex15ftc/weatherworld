# Billboard texture review (2026-10-25)

This review compares the lab's billboard storms with the reference photos (`docs/storm-reference/real/`, contact sheets `frames/real_sheet1-3.jpg`) and the Severity clips (`docs/storm-reference/frames/twisted_sheet.jpg`, `docs/tornado-reference/frames/clip_mobile_severity_sheet.jpg`). Lab captures were taken in Studio on the same day. It covers what to change and keeps everything as billboards. **Steps 1, 2, 3 (curtain texture + slant; the separate rain-foot card was folded into the texture), 5 and the colour item are implemented (2026-10-25, see STATUS.md). Not yet: torn scud sheet and background-relative scud shade (4), anvil sheet, mammatus shading, crisp shelf edge (6).**

## The root causes (they explain most of what "doesn't line up")

1. **Every card is one flat colour.** The `*Flat` textures carry their shape in alpha only, and the whole card gets one `ImageColor3`. A cloud made of flat-coloured silhouettes has no shading inside a card. The light changes only from card to card, so each card's outline shows as a hard-edged patch of colour. That is why the base reads as a pile of separate ovals and the mammatus as dots.
   - *Change:* give cards shading inside them with a `UIGradient` per card (a Color sequence: lighter on the side toward the light, darker away). For flat underside cards the gradient runs vertically; for tower cards it runs along sun·normal. No new asset is needed, and the gradient direction comes from the same normals and sun direction already computed.
2. **Only 16 shapes, all soft round puffs.** Each 4×4 sheet gives 16 frames, all the same family (round puff with a soft rim). With 1,000+ cards the repeats show. Every edge in the storm is equally soft, while real storms mix soft edges (precipitation, undersides) with crisp ones (sunlit towers, the anvil's top, a shelf's leading edge, a cloud's edge against a bright background).
   - *Change:* a second texture family with crisp edges for those places; more shapes per family.
3. **One texture is asked to look like five different things.** Base, scud, anvil, mammatus and rain all reuse the general puff sheets. Each real feature has a characteristic texture (below), and that is where the visible mismatch is.

## Colour, not texture (worth noting)

- **Undersides:** real ones are **blue-slate or teal** (lit by blue sky; `03_hp_b`, `04_c`, `06_*`, `09_a`). Ours are neutral grey. The cards' sky term should use the sky's colour, not a neutral grey.
- **Sunlit cloud:** real sunlit cloud is **warm cream**. Ours is white-grey. The sun term should carry the sun's colour (it already warms at a low sun).
- **Brightest place under a storm:** usually the rain or the clear slot behind it (`06_clear_slot_*`, `11_a`). That contrast is what makes scud and the funnel readable, and it comes from the rain-curtain and fog colours, not from the base.

## Suggested order

1. **Per-card gradient shading** (no assets; fixes the "separate ovals" look everywhere).
2. **Base deck from large seamless cards** (`ceiling` tile, already uploaded).
3. **Rain curtain texture + wind tilt + rain foot** (one new asset).
4. **Scud sizes, stretch and background-relative shade** (code), then the torn-fragment sheet.
5. **Mesocyclone striation band** (one new asset; also reused on the wall cloud and funnel).
6. **Anvil sheet**, mammatus shading, crisp shelf edge, sky- and sun-coloured light.

New assets would be made by the existing Blender and Python tools (`tools/smoke/`, `tools/blender/`) as white-with-alpha sheets and uploaded only with your OK.
