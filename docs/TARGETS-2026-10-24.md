## Forecast changes later on 2026-10-24

**Day 3 from the ingredients for storms** (DayOneOutlook `INGREDIENTS`, `stormShare`):
- The four Day 2 runs go on to Day 3 without storms. At each place they add the storm-start rate
  (InitiationEngine `diagnoseInitiation`) times a severity factor from MLCAPE and 0-6 km shear. Summed
  within 25 miles over the day, that gives a Poisson chance of a severe storm nearby.
- Cost: about 4 x 8 s against the old 2 x 14 s of storm runs.
- The raw chance nearly verified as itself (35% verified 33%, 45% verified 46%) before calibration.

| Check | Right | Within one |
|---|---|---|
| Fit set, before its calibration | 52% | 87% |
| Seeds 21-30 (10 outlooks) | 50% | 80% |

**Update calibration** (`day1update`, used from `updateAfterHours` 3 into the day): fitted on 100 update
issues (seeds 1-10 x 5, 18Z and 00Z). On seeds 31-40 it hardly moves the day's category:

| Issue | With the table | Without |
|---|---|---|
| 18Z | 37% right (27% too high) | 37% right (30% too high) |
| 00Z | 63% right | 67% right |

It is kept for the contours' reliability. On these seeds the 18Z misses run both ways (27% high,
37% low).

**Day 1's lean.** The areas match: Enhanced or more covers 30% of the map in forecasts against 31%
verified, Moderate or more 16% against 17%. The miss is the day's peak:

| | Forecast | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| High | 4% | 13% |
| Moderate | 44% | 30% |

The top tornado contours under-verify (45% verified 57%, 60% verified 80%), and EF3+ hatching (needed
for High at 30%) is seldom drawn. This is the agreement rule (top contour from 6 of 8 runs): the
runs' mean peak is below any one day's. The decision is with the user.

## (b) Wind groups and (c) hail-driven Moderate days (2026-10-24)

**(b)** 5.4% of wind reports reach 75 mph (6,373 reports, seeds 1-4 x 5 days), about the US share.
Storm gusts reach 75 mph in 17% of severe samples. The wind CIG1 bar (42% of an area's reports at
75 mph+) was set on the old report model and almost no area reached it. The frequency fit
(`scripts/cig-climate.mjs`, aim 30/10/3) wanted 0.02, which is meaningless.

Bars judged offline on that run's 40 days:

| Wind bars | CIG1+ / 2+ / 3+ days | High | Moderate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.42 / 0.32 / 0.22 (before) | 3 / 0 / 0% | 8% | 25% |
| 0.20 / 0.08 / 0.03 (set) | 13 / 3 / 0% | 10% | 23% |

**(c)** The hail CIG1 bar is on its aim (2 in hatching verifies on 38% of days, aim 35%; the fit
wants 0.22 against 0.20), so it is unchanged. Hail-driven Moderate days are 60% hail coverage with 2
in hatching, which is a Moderate by the SPC matrix. The coverage comes from the dense reports of
the broad outbreak days, the same breadth as the High days, not a separate hail fault.

The outlook's wind CIG shares (CIG_SHARE) had too few areas to fit before; the next refit will
fit them against the new bars.
