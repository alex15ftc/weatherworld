## Status Rules

- `STATUS.md` is the compact source of truth for current framing and operating state.
- Limits: whole file under 6 KB; any single bullet under 80 words; `Recently Completed` holds the last 10 items. When something does not fit, move depth to `STRATEGY_MASTER.md`, unsettled reasoning to `STRATEGY_WORKING.md`, milestones to `HISTORY.md`, and detail to a memo or work folder, then link it. Never compress prose to fit.
- `Goals` are strategic outcomes or questions still being resolved. `Action Items` are concrete executable tasks only.
- `Recently Completed` is for completed action items only. Drop the oldest when adding an eleventh; anything that was a milestone belongs in `HISTORY.md`.
- Do not mark anything complete unless it has actually been completed and explicitly confirmed.

## Current Framing SOT

WeatherWorld is a shared-weather, open-world storm-chasing MMO on Roblox. One deterministic weather engine runs the weather for every Roblox server, so every player chases the same storms; the engine's storms are drawn in 3-D from its own fields. The rest of the framing (audience, positioning) is for the owner to write.
