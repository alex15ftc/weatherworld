# Research

Optional. Sourced external research and reference material that outlives any single effort: market maps, competitor profiles, regulatory context.

- Name files `YYYY-MM-DD_TITLE.md` and cite sources; recheck external claims before consequential use.
- Our own analysis and decision memos go in `../memos/`; research produced for one effort can stay in that effort's work folder.
- Research is not current strategy. Promote accepted conclusions into `STRATEGY_MASTER.md` and summarize active implications in `STATUS.md`.
