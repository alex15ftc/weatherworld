The large-scale model is a channel 10,240 km around (it wraps east-west) and 5,120 km north-south, with the jet stream's core along its middle. The game map (the box) reads its weather from wherever it sits and drifts along the wave train to meet the day's weather. These are the large-scale fields: the map's own analysis adds daytime heating, the lee trough, cold pools and storms.
