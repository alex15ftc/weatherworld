The large-scale model is a channel 10,240 km around (it wraps east-west) and 5,120 km north-south, with the jet stream's core along its middle. The game map is the box: the chart follows it east-west; drag or zoom the chart and the box moves through the times ("Map" follows it again). These are the large-scale fields: the map's own analysis adds daytime heating, the lee trough, cold pools and storms.
